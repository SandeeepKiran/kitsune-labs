# 01 - Product brief

## Who it is for

1. **Recruiters and hiring managers** (main audience). They spend 1-3 minutes. They must see,
   without reading much, that the owner builds real QA tooling for conversational AI.
2. **QA engineers** working on chatbots or voice bots, who may actually use the tools.

## What a visitor must be able to do

1. Open the site and understand what it is in about 5 seconds.
2. Open any tool and click **"Load Mousy sample"** to see it work immediately: no upload,
   no key, no sign-up.
3. Download the sample files (transcripts, Excel batches, intent lists) to try the bulk features.
4. Optionally paste their own API key (Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude) to unlock the AI features,
   and see clearly that the key stays in their browser.

## What it must feel like

- **Senior QA engineering software first**: dense, precise, keyboard-friendly, honest numbers.
- **Subtle kitsune theme second**: Yae Miko colours, a small fox mark, a few quiet details.
  Never game-like. Never "receptionist / front desk".

## Out of scope (for now)

- Accounts, logins, a backend, a database, payments.
- Any connection to a real bot platform. Tools work on files and pasted text only.

## Success looks like

- A recruiter can open 3 tools and see results in under 2 minutes, with no key.
- Zero employer or client information anywhere in the repo (verified by a privacy pass
  before the repo goes public, like the Leyline Autofill launch).
